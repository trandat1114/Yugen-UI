# Refraction for glass, cherry blossom season in the Glass template

The edges of glass surfaces bend the image behind them like the rim of a lens (GlassRefraction, Chromium browsers). The Glass template adds Kyoto, Tokyo, Hirosaki and Mount Fuji in blossom season. Adds Sonner (stacked notifications) and four display components: Timeline, DescriptionList, Stat and Prose. The Templates page has a full-screen mode; Checkbox, Switch and Dropdown in a Field no longer stretch the page when they sit in a form inside a scrolling area. Yugen Labs adds a boot loading screen (a calligraphy brush stroke, kanji or Latin numerals) and HTTP status pages (404, 500, maintenance, offline…).

## New

- Sonner: notifications that stack in a corner of the screen and fan out on hover or focus. Call `sonner("…")` or `sonner.success / info / warning / error / loading` from anywhere; `sonner.promise` switches in place from loading to success or error; call again with the same `id` to update a notification. Action and cancel buttons, `richColors`, swipe to dismiss, F8 to reach the notification region. Built on Radix Toast, with no new dependency. [→](/components/sonner)
- Timeline (history, activity logs; detailed and compact), DescriptionList (label / value pairs, drops columns when narrow, copy button), Stat (headline number, delta, a small chart; StatTile is now built on Stat) and Prose (typography for Markdown or HTML from a CMS, opt out with `not-prose`). [→](/components/timeline)
- GlassRefraction (Yugen Labs): place it once at the app root and the edges of every glass surface bend the background behind them like the rim of a lens, while the middle stays frosted so text keeps its contrast. Only Chromium browsers draw it; Safari and Firefox keep the regular glass. It turns off along with the blur under reduced transparency, increased contrast, forced colors and the high-contrast theme. [→](/foundations/glass)
- Slider and Switch gain `material="glass"` (Yugen Labs): while pressed or dragged, the thumb turns to glass, growing larger on Slider so the track shows through it, and longer on Switch. [→](/components/slider)
- Boot loading screen (Yugen Labs): a brush stroke in the middle follows real loading progress, kanji numerals 〇–百 run vertically down the edge, and the ensō logo is inked in the corner and blooms when loading is done. It appears in `npm run dev`, on `404.html` and on prerendered pages that stay hidden because their language or RTL direction doesn't match. The Labs page has a lab for loading screen concepts in two numeral styles, kanji and Latin, viewable full screen. [→](/concepts/loading-screen)
- HTTP status pages (Yugen Labs): 400, 401, 403, 404, 410, 429, 500, 503, 504, offline and new version, in the same layout as the loading screen, with a logo that changes with the status and a countdown that follows `Retry-After`. Comes with a status catalog and how each page recovers on its own. [→](/concepts/status-pages)

## Improved

- The Blog and Docs templates style articles with Prose instead of separate classes on each element. [→](/templates)
- Glass MenuPopover and PopoverSurface grow out of the button that opens them and shrink back into it when they close; with reduced motion they still just fade. [→](/foundations/glass)
- Theme Designer: a Glass axis (airy, default, frosted) and brand-tinted glass, previewed over a photo, with a check that text on glass stays at 4.5:1 or more over a black or white backdrop; the exported CSS includes the glass variables. [→](/theme-designer)
- The Glass template: four cherry blossom trips to Kyoto, Tokyo, Hirosaki and around Mount Fuji (Wikimedia Commons photos, credited with their licenses); the Settings page previews bars and controls over a bright photo, a blossom photo and a night photo; the Explore page is a world map (Natural Earth) with each trip pinned at its real coordinates, and nearby pins group together and zoom in when selected. [→](/templates)
- Templates page: a full-screen button on each preview, with desktop, tablet or phone sizes; a `?preview=<template>` link opens this view directly. The other templates are picked with tabs and shown in one frame as large as the featured template; a `#<template>` link preselects a template. [→](/templates)
- Notice: React 18.3 stays supported for the rest of 0.x; from 1.0, Yugen UI supports React 19 only. If your app still uses React 18, move to React 19 before updating to 1.0. [→](/concepts/quick-start)

## Fixed

- Checkbox, Switch and Dropdown in a Field: inside a `<form>` in a scrolling area, the hidden input used for form submission no longer stretches the page and leaves blank space at the bottom. [→](/components/checkbox)
