# Glass template: cherry blossom season in Japan

The Glass template adds four trips in Japan, refraction at the glass edges, a photo preview on the Settings page and a world map on the Explore page. The Blog and Docs templates style articles with Prose.

## Improved

- `npx create-yugen-ui@latest my-app --template glass`: trips to Kyoto, Tokyo, Hirosaki and around Mount Fuji, GlassRefraction at the app root, a Settings page that previews the glass over a bright photo, a blossom photo and a night photo, and an Explore page with a world map pinned at real coordinates. The template pins `yugen-ui@^0.13.0`. [→](/templates)
- Blog and Docs templates: Markdown articles are styled with Prose from `yugen-ui` instead of classes on every tag. [→](/components/prose)
