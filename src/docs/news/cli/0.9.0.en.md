# Registry items checked against the CLI's own checksums

`add`, `update` and the other registry commands read the registry of the `yugen-ui` version your templates use and check every item against a sha256 shipped inside the CLI, before writing any file. The templates move to `yugen-ui` 0.17.

## New

- `add`, `init`, `update`, `diff`, `list` and `remove` read `<docs>/r/<version>/` for the `yugen-ui` version of the templates and compare each item with `dist/registry-integrity.json`. A mismatch stops before anything is written. A registry given with `--registry` or in `yugen.json` / `components.json` is not checked, and the CLI says so. [→](/concepts/cli)

## Improved

- `yugen css` and `init` add `yugen-ui/tokens/brands.css` when the app uses the web or Teams brand, which `theme-core.css` no longer carries. [→](/concepts/cli#css)
- Templates on `yugen-ui` 0.17: `vite-admin` validates its forms with `HookForm` and `standardSchemaResolver` from `yugen-ui/form`, `vite-dashboard` and `vite-saas` import the date components from `yugen-ui/dates` with `react-day-picker`, and `vite-store` styles its radio cards with `has-checked:`. [→](/templates)
