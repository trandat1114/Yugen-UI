Charts: moving the pointer over LineChart, AreaChart and BarChart no longer recomputes the chart (#352). Scales, paths, the screen reader label of every point and the layers that do not change on hover are kept until the data, the props or the width change, and the points share one set of event handlers, so a hover step only redraws the crosshair, the tooltip and, for bars, the bars whose dimming changes. A hover step goes from 9.7 to 1.1 ms at 500 points and from 39 to 1.1 ms at 2,000 (three series, jsdom), and a keyboard step from 43 to 9.6 ms at 2,000. Colors shared by a series (line markers, bars) and the gridlines are set once on the series group instead of on every mark: a 4 × 200 LineChart with markers has 16 inline styles instead of 1,014 (−30% markup), a 60 × 4 BarChart 13 instead of 490 (−14%). The drawn pixels, keyboard navigation and screen reader output are unchanged; points now carry a `data-i` attribute. `{ BarChart, LineChart }` grows from 30.2 to 31.1 kB gzip. New `useElementSize({ enabled })`: with `false` the hook measures nothing and adds no ResizeObserver. Sparkline passes `enabled: false` when `width` is a number, so 200 fixed-width Sparklines mount in one render instead of three, with no observers.
